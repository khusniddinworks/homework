1-Qism: Writing Task 1 (Pebbleton Maps)

Xaritalarni tasvirlash qoidalari (Eslatma)

Xaritalardagi o'zgarishlarni tasvirlashda odatda Present Perfect Passive (has been built, have been demolished) ishlatiladi.

  • Qurish/Qo'shish: construct, build, erect, introduce, add to.
  • Buzib tashlash/Olib tashlash: demolish, knock down, pull down, cut down, remove.
  • Kattalashtirish/Kengaytirish: expand, extend, enlarge, develop.
  • O'zgartirish: transform, turn into, convert, replace, modernize.

Quyidagi Pebbleton shaharchasining 20 yil oldingi va hozirgi xaritasini solishtiring va insho yozing.

Model Answer (Namuna):

The maps illustrate the developments that have taken place in the coastal town of Pebbleton over a 20-year period. Overall, the town has undergone significant transformation, evolving from a historic and relatively undeveloped area into a more modern residential and recreational hub, while the school and lighthouse have remained unchanged.

Looking at the northern part of the town, twenty years ago there was an old fort situated near the lighthouse. This has since been demolished and replaced by a children’s playground. Additionally, a new community centre has been constructed adjacent to the existing houses on Lighthouse Lane, and the residential area itself has been expanded with the addition of more houses along the coast.

In the central and southern areas, the film studio that once stood near the coast has been knocked down to make way for a block of flats. A new footpath now connects these flats to Lighthouse Lane, and a footbridge has been built across the river, providing direct access from the flats to the Coast Road near the school. Furthermore, tennis courts have been erected right next to the playing field. To accommodate these new developments, many of the trees in the town have been cut down.
